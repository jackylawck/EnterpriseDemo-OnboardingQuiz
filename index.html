📌 專案摘要與合規定位 📌 Executive Summary & Compliance Positioning

本儲存庫為一項企業管治示範專案，展示如何以輕量級 Streamlit 介面、動態 Secrets 隔離、JSON Schema 資料校驗與確定性「治理即代碼」（Governance-as-Code）構建新員工入職培訓考核與滿意度調研閉環。

This repository is an Enterprise Demo demonstrating a Zero-Data-Retention new-hire onboarding assessment, engagement survey, and controlled archiving workflow built with Streamlit, isolated Cloud Secrets, JSON Schemas, and deterministic Governance-as-Code.

傳統紙本入職測驗與問卷常面臨批改滯後、問卷數據難以統計、紙本易遺失及審計抽查耗時等痛點。EnterpriseDemo-OnboardingQuiz 依據國際管理體系（ISO 9001 / ISO 45001 / ISO/IEC 27001）設計，在不架設雲端資料庫、零個人資料持久化（Zero-Data-Retention）的前提下，整合「入職規章考核」、「培訓滿意度評估」與「文康／ESG 義工興趣調查」，即時於記憶體動態生成受控繁體中文 PDF 報告（DEMO-HR-REC-2026-V1），透過後端 TLS SMTP 一鍵加密直送人力資源部（HRD），並對接下游歸檔與不合格補考追蹤工作流。

Paper-based induction quizzes and onboarding surveys suffer from slow manual grading, fragmented feedback, missing timestamps, and audit overhead. EnterpriseDemo-OnboardingQuiz aligns with international management standards (ISO 9001 / ISO 45001 / ISO/IEC 27001). Without provisioning any external database, employee responses remain strictly in volatile session memory to integrate policy assessment, training satisfaction ratings, and ESG/wellness engagement surveys into a controlled PDF record (DEMO-HR-REC-2026-V1), dispatched via TLS SMTP to HR and paired with downstream archiving and retraining workflows.

在法規邊界定性上，本系統完全採用確定性規則比對邏輯（Deterministic Rule-Based Logic），不含任何 AI / 機器學習模型或自動化人事決策演算法，明確排除於《歐盟人工智能法案》（EU AI Act）及 ISO/IEC 42001 強制範圍之外，同時全面對齊香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》（第 486 章）保障資料原則（DPP 1–4）。

From a regulatory boundary perspective, this application uses strictly deterministic rule-based scoring logic (Non-AI) with zero machine learning or automated employment profiling—explicitly scoping out EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001 obligations while adhering to Hong Kong PDPO (Cap. 486) Data Protection Principles (DPP 1–4).

⚙️ 技術亮點與合規工程實踐 ⚙️ Technical & Compliance Engineering Highlights

1. 零資料留存架構Zero-Data-Retention

無外置資料庫或雲端檔案桶，個人資料與問卷僅於記憶體暫存用於單次 PDF 渲染，發送完畢立即釋放，從根源杜絕外洩。No external DB or cloud bucket; PII and survey responses reside strictly in session RAM for one-time PDF rendering before purge.

2. 動態 Secrets 機密隔離Dynamic Secrets Isolation

題庫答案、門禁通行碼與 SMTP 憑證均嚴格封裝於雲端環境變數，公開倉庫 100% 零機密硬編碼，支援熱更新試題。Quiz answer keys, access tokens, and SMTP credentials live solely in Cloud Secrets—zero hardcoded secrets with hot-swappable banks.

3. JSON Schema 驗證與消毒JSON Schema & Sanitization

以標準 Draft-07 Schema 定義欄位型別與長度上限，並過濾換行及 \/*?:"<>| 危險字元，防範注入與路徑穿越。Enforces Draft-07 Schema type/length limits and sanitizes control/path characters to block injection and path traversal attacks.

4. 香港時區標準化存證HKT Timezone Normalization

底層統一鎖定 UTC+8 香港標準時間（HKT）蓋印 PDF 報告與審計紀錄，滿足 ISO 9001 及本地人力資源合規稽核要求。Standardizes all PDF timestamps to Hong Kong Time (UTC+8 HKT) to guarantee consistent IMS and local HR audit traceability.

5. 確定性非 AI 治理邊界Non-AI Regulatory Scope-Out

刻意採用透明確定性規則評分，不含任何 AI/ML 模型或員工剖析演算法，主動排除 EU AI Act 及 ISO/IEC 42001 監管負擔。Deliberately utilizes transparent deterministic scoring with zero AI/ML profiling, cleanly scoping out EU AI Act and ISO 42001.

6. 雙階段自動化工作流藍圖Dual-Stage Workflow Blueprints

配套 Flow1（TLS SMTP 加密直寄）與 Flow2（受控人事庫歸檔及不合格 7 天補考分流），建構端對端無紙化閉環。Pairs Flow1 (TLS SMTP dispatch) with Flow2 (restricted HR IMS archiving and 7-day fail retraining trigger) for full closure.
